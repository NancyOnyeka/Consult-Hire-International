      Engagement Terms

      1. Fee Structure

      Our placement fees are calculated as a percentage of the successful candidate's first-year remuneration. Fees are payable upon candidate commencement.

      2. Replacement Guarantee

      We offer a 3-month replacement guarantee for all permanent placements. If the candidate leaves or is terminated within this period, we will conduct a replacement search at no additional fee.

      3. Exclusivity

      For retained searches, we require a period of exclusivity. For contingent searches, we operate on a first-introduction basis.

      4. Confidentiality

      All candidate information shared by Consult & Hire is confidential and proprietary. Client companies agree not to disclose candidate details to third parties or approach candidates directly without our consent.

      5. Refund Policy

      In the unlikely event that a placed candidate does not commence employment, any fees paid will be refunded in full within 30 days.
