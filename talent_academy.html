    The Cost of Getting It Wrong

    A single hire can make or break an organisation.

    A poor hiring decision can reduce productivity, weaken team morale, affect customers, damage leadership credibility and consume significant time and financial resources. The true cost is rarely confined to recruitment fees or salary.

    Time Investment

    Lost leadership time, repeated interviews, delayed projects and the cost of restarting the hiring process.

    Financial Investment

    Salary, onboarding, agency costs, training, missed revenue and replacement expenditure.

    Organisational Impact

    Culture, customer confidence, employee retention, team performance and employer reputation.
